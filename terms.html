Piki Kiki is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. An ad can play only after you tap "Retry Level After Ad", at most once for every two retries and never less than 90 seconds after the previous ad. 4. Gems and boosters

Gems are earned by scoring in the game and have no cash value. They are spent in the in-game shop on boosters. Gems and boosters cannot be bought with money, transferred to other people or refunded, and the game has no purchases made with real money.

5. No account; iCloud sync

You do not need an account. If iCloud is on for Piki Kiki, the game tries to keep your progress, gems, best score and settings in your own iCloud storage so you can continue on another device. This is best-effort and has limits: two devices played offline are merged (the highest level and best score are kept, earned and spent gems are added up, and the same gems spent on two devices can leave a balance of zero); reinstalling without iCloud cannot restore progress; synchronisation depends on Apple's service.
