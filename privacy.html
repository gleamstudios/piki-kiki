Piki Kiki Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Who we are

Piki Kiki is a free puzzle game for iPhone, published by Kevin Weiss, trading as Gleam Studios. Kevin Weiss is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: factiveapp@pm.me.

2. No account

You do not register, sign in or create an account, and the game does not use Sign in with Apple. We do not run a game server.

3. What stays on your device

Your level progress, gem balance, best score, shop purchases (made with gems you earn in play, never with money), ad-frequency counters and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

4. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for Piki Kiki, the game also saves your progress, gems, best score and settings in your own iCloud storage so you can continue on another of your devices. This data goes to Apple's iCloud under your Apple ID, not to us, and we cannot see it. If iCloud is off or unavailable, the game works fully offline. Reinstalling without iCloud cannot restore your progress.

5. Analytics (Google Firebase)

Piki Kiki uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how the game is played so we can improve it. Where the law requires your consent (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), analytics starts only after you agree. The game sends game events such as levels started, completed and failed, scores and moves, gems earned and spent, booster use, and what happened at ad opportunities. Firebase also creates its own app-instance identifier and receives device and app information such as device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate region derived from your IP address. We do not send your name, e-mail address, contacts or free text, and the game does not use your advertising identifier for analytics. You can switch analytics off at any time in Privacy & Ads in the game.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. An ad can play only after you tap "Retry Level After Ad" following a failed level, at most once for every two retries and never less than 90 seconds after the previous ad. If an ad cannot load, you can still retry the level. Google may collect device identifiers, your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics to serve, measure and protect ads. We ask AdMob to limit ads to a general-audience content rating; we do not control individual ads. You can report an inappropriate ad from Privacy & Ads in the game or by e-mail. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. Consent and your choices

  • Where the law requires it, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform appears before ads or analytics start. You can change your choice later using "Ad privacy choices" in Privacy & Ads, where it is offered.
  • After you finish your first level, iOS may ask whether Piki Kiki may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, ads can be personalised using your advertising identifier. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised, and the game works the same. You can change this in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Privacy & Ads in the game lets you turn analytics off, change ad privacy choices where offered, report an ad, and open this policy and the support page.

8. Children

Piki Kiki is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age set by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your data for money. Information goes to Google (Firebase Analytics and AdMob) as described above, and to Apple as part of iCloud and the App Store. Google and Apple may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

10. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Your iCloud copy stays until you remove it in iOS Settings (your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage). Analytics data is kept only for the limited retention period we set in Google Analytics. Ad-related data is kept by Google under its own policies. To ask us to delete analytics data tied to your device's app-instance identifier, e-mail us.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to it, to receive it in a portable form, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Because we hold no account and no directly identifying information about you, we may need your device's app-instance identifier to find analytics data about you. E-mail us to use these rights.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
